# Pricing Overview

IoTDataHub offers a **free tier with no credit card required**, plus transparent usage-based pricing as you scale.

## Free tier

The free tier includes everything you need to get started:

- Connect devices via [Arduino library](/connect-devices/arduino-library) or [REST API](/connect-devices/rest-api)
- Up to **8 fields per channel**
- Minimum **15-second** update interval
- [Dashboards](/dashboards) and email [alerts](/alerts)

[Get started free →](https://www.iotdatahub.rw/)

## Pro plan

When your project needs faster updates or SMS alerts:

| Feature | Pro |
| ------- | --- |
| Fields per channel | 8 |
| Minimum update rate | 1 second |
| SMS alerts | Yes |
