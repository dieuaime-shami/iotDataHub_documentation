# Field Limits & Plans

Each IoTDataHub channel supports a defined number of fields and update rates depending on your plan.

## Limits by plan

| Plan | Fields per channel | Minimum update rate |
| ---- | ------------------ | ------------------- |
| Free | 8 fields | 15 seconds |
| Pro | 8 fields | 1 second |

## Free tier

A **free tier is available — no credit card required**. It includes everything you need to connect devices, stream data, and build dashboards.

[Get started free on IoTDataHub →](https://www.iotdatahub.rw/)

## Scaling up

When your project grows beyond the free tier, IoTDataHub offers **transparent, usage-based pricing** with no surprises. View full plan details on the [Pricing page](https://www.iotdatahub.rw/pricing).

## Tips

- Use all 8 fields efficiently — combine related readings on one channel
- On the free tier, set `delay(15000)` or higher in Arduino `loop()` to avoid rate limits
- Pro plan unlocks faster updates and SMS alerts — see [Alerts & Automation](/alerts)
