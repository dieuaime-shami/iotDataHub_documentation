# Dashboards

Build interactive dashboards with line charts, gauges, and bar graphs. Slice historical data by time range and export reports in one click.

IoTDataHub supports dashboard views for **Environmental**, **Energy**, and **Network** analytics — matching the live platform preview at [iotdatahub.rw](https://www.iotdatahub.rw/).

| Guide | Description |

| [Time Ranges & Export](/dashboards/time-ranges) | Filter history and download reports |
| [Sharing Dashboards](/dashboards/sharing) | Share read-only links with your team |

## Create your first dashboard

1. Navigate to **Dashboards** in your IoTDataHub account
2. Click **New Dashboard** and choose a template or start blank
3. Add widgets by selecting your [channels](/data-channels/channels) and fields

Pair dashboards with [Smart Alerts](/alerts) for proactive monitoring.
