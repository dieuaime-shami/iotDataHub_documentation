# Connect Devices

Register your IoT devices and control them from the cloud. IoTDataHub supports **Arduino**, **ESP32**, **ESP8266**, **Raspberry Pi**, and any HTTP-capable hardware.

## How it works

```
Create Channel  →  Register Device  →  Build Widget Box  →  Upload Sketch  →  Control Online
```

Each device gets a virtual control panel (Widget Box) that mirrors your physical hardware. Actions on the dashboard are sent to your microcontroller in real time.

New here? Follow the complete walkthrough in [Getting Started](/getting-started).

## Quick start

1. [Create a channel](/data-channels/channels) with your hardware and connection type
2. Register a device from that channel on the **Devices** page
3. Build your [Widget Box](/dashboards/widgets) by dragging widgets onto the canvas
4. Copy your [device configuration](/connect-devices/arduino-library#device-configuration) and paste it into your Arduino sketch
5. Upload the sketch — your device goes online and you can control it from the browser
